Caveats
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Model pairs are not independent: every model enters many pairs, and models of one family share architecture, data and recipe. The 378 EC–EC and 55 NEC–NEC correlations are summarised descriptively and never treated as independent observations.
* Every family is entirely EC or entirely NEC (5 EC families, 9 NEC families, each NEC family a single checkpoint), so EC/NEC is confounded with family.
* NEC models differ in focus language (de, fr, pt, zh, ja) and training type (continual pretraining, fine-tuning, from scratch); lower NEC–NEC agreement may reflect this heterogeneity rather than the absence of English-centric training as such.
* Correlation of preferences is not correctness: two models can agree on wrong preferences.

ACL-ready paragraph (descriptive)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To describe whether English-centric (EC) models agree more with each other than non-English-centric (NEC)
models do, we grouped the pairwise Spearman correlations between the models' sentence-pair-level ΔSLOR into
EC–EC (28 models, 378 pairs) and NEC–NEC (11 models, 55 pairs) model pairs. The median EC–EC correlation (ρ =
0.71, IQR 0.28–0.78) was higher than the median NEC–NEC correlation (ρ = 0.52, IQR 0.38–0.68; EC–NEC pairs:
0.57), Δmedianρ = 0.19. EC models from different families correlated with a median ρ = 0.71 (291 pairs; same-
family EC pairs: 0.73), so agreement among EC models is not confined to models of one family. Giving every
pair of families equal weight changed the difference to 0.16. With only 5 EC families and 9 single-checkpoint
NEC models, these correlations describe the evaluated models and do not show that English-centric training
causes higher agreement.

Sensitivity model sets (different models; NOT equivalent to the ALL analysis; ALL_without_gemma4_it is post hoc, chosen after seeing that the five Gemma 4 IT checkpoints form the low EC–EC tail, and must be reported as exploratory):

  ALL_without_manaca (28 EC / 10 NEC): EC–EC 0.710, NEC–NEC 0.572 (45 pairs), Δ = 0.137
